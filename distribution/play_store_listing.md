## 📄 Full Description (4,000 characters max)

You are never alone. **HearU** is a privacy-first emotional wellness platform created to ensure that whenever life feels heavy, overwhelming, or lonely, you have a safe, judgment-free space to be heard.

### 🌟 Key Features

#### 1. 👥 100% Anonymous Peer-to-Peer Listening
- **Connect with Compassionate Listeners:** Express what is on your mind without fear of judgment.
- **Emotion Matching:** Tag what you're feeling (*Anxiety, Loneliness, Work Stress, Relationships, Grief, Insomnia, or Just Venting*) to find empathetic peers ready to listen.
- **Mutual Photo Privacy:** Photos stay cryptographically blurred by default. Both parties must mutually agree in-chat to reveal avatars.

#### 2. 🎤 Real-Time Audio Voice Notes
- **Speak Your Truth:** Record warm, calming voice notes directly inside the conversation.
- **Dynamic Waveform Visualizer:** Visual audio cues that bring genuine human connection without demanding real identities.

#### 3. 🤖 24/7 AI Companion (Powered by Gemini 3.7 Flash)
- **Always Available:** For 3:00 AM thoughts, late-night worries, or quick emotional processing.
- **Built-in Safety Guardrails:** Continuous empathy scoring with automatic detection of crisis signals.

#### 4. 🌬️ 4-7-8 Breathing & 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Tools
- **Instant Panic & Anxiety Relief:** Follow the soothing animated breathing orb (*Inhale 4s, Hold 7s, Exhale 8s*) to activate the parasympathetic nervous system.
- **Sensory Grounding:** Step-by-step guidance to re-anchor your mind to the present moment.

#### 5. 🚨 24/7 Emergency & Crisis Lifeline (SOS)
- **1-Tap Direct Access:** Immediate dialing and SMS routing to the **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**, Crisis Text Line (741741), The Trevor Project, and Veterans Crisis Line.

#### 6. 🔒 Absolute Privacy & 30-Day Auto Purge Guarantee
- **Zero Permanent Storage:** All peer and AI chat transcripts are permanently purged from all servers every 30 days.
- **Biometric App Lock:** Secure your personal haven with Face ID / Fingerprint authentication.
- **No Data Selling:** HearU never monetizes or sells private user transcripts.
