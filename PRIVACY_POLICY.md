# Privacy Policy for HearU
**Effective Date:** September 1, 2026

## 1. Introduction
HearU ("we", "our", or "us") provides a platform designed to foster supportive peer emotional connections. Your privacy and safety are our highest priorities.

## 2. Strict 18+ Age Policy
HearU is strictly restricted to individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

## 3. Data Collection & Use
- **Account Information:** Email and verified phone number (used solely for anti-abuse and authentication).
- **Profile Details:** Display name, emotion tags, and trust rating. Real names and email addresses are never made public.
- **Photos:** Profile photos remain cryptographically blurred and are only unblurred upon mutual, explicit in-session consent.
- **Biometrics:** Biometric data (Fingerprint/Face ID) remains exclusively within the secure enclave of your Android device and is never transmitted to our servers.

## 4. 30-Day Ephemeral Chat Retention
To uphold maximum privacy and minimize data exposure, all human-to-human peer chat logs and AI conversation transcripts are permanently purged from our databases 30 days after creation via automated deletion jobs.

## 5. AI Interactions (Gemini 3.7 Flash)
Conversations with the AI Companion are processed securely via the Firebase AI Logic SDK. Data is strictly processed for the conversational session and is governed by responsible AI safety filters.

## 6. Safety & Crisis Escalation
HearU employs automated keyword heuristics and crisis protocols. If indicators of immediate self-harm, emergency distress, or violence are detected, we surface direct 24/7 hotline resources (988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, 112/911).

## 7. Contact & Support
For data deletion requests or moderation inquiries, contact: `privacy@hearu.app`.
